# Privacy Policy — Ank Dhara (DRAFT)

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This is a draft; it must be reviewed and hosted at a public web address before the Play Store listing.

**App:** Ank Dhara (package `com.ankdhara.numerology`)

## What the app stores
- Name, date of birth and other details you type for profiles, saved **on your device only**.
- Settings (language, theme, numerology style) on your device.
- Signature photos you pick are analysed on your device and are not uploaded.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not create accounts or collect your phone number or contacts.

## Ads and purchases (when enabled)
- Free version may show Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use an advertising ID; you will be asked for consent where required.
- Paid reports and the ad-free plan are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see your card details.

## Your control
- You can delete profiles inside the app, or clear the app data / uninstall to remove everything.

## Disclaimer
Numerology, graphology and health notes are traditional/entertainment guidance, not scientific or medical advice. Please consult a doctor for health matters.
