Ank Dhara - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

App: Ank Dhara (com.ankdhara.numerology), a numerology app (screens in 11 languages; long texts and PDF reports in English and Hindi).

What the app stores

  • Names, dates of birth, phone and house numbers that you type, and your settings. They are saved on your phone only.
  • A signature photo you choose is analysed on your phone and is not uploaded.

Things that use the internet

Voice input uses the speech service of your phone (usually Google), which may send your voice to that service to turn it into text. If you send feedback from the app, only the message you write is sent. Please do not write private details in it.

Backup of the app data to cloud services is turned off.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your contacts.

Ads and purchases (when turned on)

  • The free version may show Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use an advertising ID; where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first.
  • Paid reports and the ad-free plan are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your card details.

Your control

You can delete profiles inside the app. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app removes everything the app saved on your phone.

Disclaimer

Numerology, graphology and health notes are traditional or entertainment guidance. They are not scientific, legal, financial or medical advice. Please see a doctor for health matters.
